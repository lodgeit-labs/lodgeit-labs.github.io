Your review time and CPD

Reviewing a rule against the Act is technical work. We send a record of it that you may be able to put towards your professional education hours.

Reviewing a rule file or a calculator against the Act is a few hours of technical tax work. You may be able to count it towards your continuing professional education.

What you will receive

The review portal we are building will issue a record of CPD activity when your verdict is accepted. Until it is live, ask and we will send one by email. It has:

  • your name and credential, as you gave them;
  • the bounty and the provisions you reviewed;
  • the date you returned your verdict;
  • the hours you declared on your verdict form (your figure, not our estimate);
  • what the activity was: a structured review of a statutory encoding against the legislation, with written findings;
  • the fingerprint (SHA-256) of your verdict file, as evidence the work was done.

Whether it counts is your call

We are not an accredited CPD provider, and this record is not accredited by any professional body or regulator.

The Tax Practitioners Board does not accredit providers. Its guidance asks that an activity be relevant to the services you provide, and that you keep a record of the date, the provider, the activity, what you learned, the hours and any evidence. The record above is designed to supply those items.

Professional associations set their own rules. Some may treat paid work differently from study. Check your association's policy, and use your own judgement. We do not promise that any body will accept the hours.
