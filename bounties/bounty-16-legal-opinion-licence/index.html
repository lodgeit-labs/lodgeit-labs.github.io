Bounty #16 · Legal opinion · OPEN · Seeking reviewer · $2,400 ex GST · about 5 hours

Open to Australian legal practitioners with a current practising certificate. Experience in copyright or technology licensing is an advantage.

What we are asking

Three questions. Open your memorandum with a one-line answer to each: yes, no, or yes with conditions.

Q1. Copyright. Does copyright subsist under the Copyright Act 1968 (Cth) in: (a) a rule file whose text was drafted by an AI system and then tested, corrected from practitioners’ findings and released by a person; and (b) the test scenarios and expected answers in that file, where each expected figure was derived by hand from the legislation before the rules were run?

Q2. Attribution without distribution. Can we effectively require attribution where a rule file is used inside someone else’s product or service and never distributed, for example supplied to a language model as background, or used to train or evaluate one? If so, which instrument does it: a copyright licence, contract terms accepted on use, or both? If not, say what the nearest enforceable requirement is.

Q3. The interim notice. Does our interim notice, Using these files, do what it says? Name anything in it that is unenforceable, or that exposes LodgeiT.

Background (enough to answer)

  • A rule file states a part of tax law in Logical English, a controlled English that a reasoner runs directly. Each file carries its rules, a comment naming the provision above each rule, and test scenarios with expected answers.
  • The files are drafted by AI systems under a person’s direction. A person runs the tests, corrects the file from paid practitioners’ written findings, and decides when it is released.
  • The files and tests are published openly. Our stated position is that anyone may read, run, test, host and build on them, that changed files must be marked as changed, and that nobody may use them silently. See Open rules, paid receipts.
  • Legislation quoted in the files is the Commonwealth’s. The reasoner is separately licensed by its authors.
  • Before 12 November 2026 the site stated no licence. The interim notice was published that day.

Deliverable

A memorandum in your own words, 1,500 to 3,000 words, in Markdown or PDF:

  1. a one-line answer to each question;
  2. reasons, citing provisions and authorities;
  3. for any "yes with conditions", the conditions, numbered;
  4. one paragraph on any worse exposure you see in the background that we have not asked about;
  5. the attestation below.

Reliance limitations and disclaimers are welcome and do not affect acceptance.

Additional points

A further $300 ex GST for each additional point we accept as material. Three we would value: who owns the copyright, if it subsists; what we must be able to prove before saying publicly that someone has used our files without credit; and whether the names of our review tiers can be protected as trade marks.

Attestation

State in the memorandum: that you hold a current Australian practising certificate; any AI tool you used and what for (drafting, research or checking); and that you verified every citation yourself. Use of AI is not disqualifying; undisclosed use is.

Acceptance

The bounty is paid when the memorandum answers all three questions with reasons tied to identifiable provisions or authorities. An answer of "no, and here is why this cannot work as described" earns the full fee. Paid on receipt of your invoice; see How we pay.

What this is not

Not a retainer, and not advice on any dispute. Not a request to draft the final licence; that is separate work that follows from the answer.

Publication

We publish the memorandum on this page with your name, or as "verified Australian legal practitioner" if you prefer, after we have verified your credential.
