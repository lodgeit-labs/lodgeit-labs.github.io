The programme

Ways to take part

Six ways to take part in an open rulebook for tax law, what each gets today, and what is designed for later.

As at 12 November 2026. We are building a public rulebook for tax law: rules a practitioner can read, a machine can run, and a second practitioner has checked. It starts in Australia. It is meant to work in any jurisdiction with its own law and its own register of practitioners.

"Today" below is what exists now. "Designed" is intent, not an offer; see From bounties to ownership.

RoleWhat you doTodayDesigned

Two people, not one

A file needs two signatures from different practitioners, at least one currently licensed, before it is called "Ratified". One signature makes it "Reviewed". None, and it stays "under review". Reviewers who work well as a pair, one finding and one signing, are welcome to say so.

Challengers

You do not need to be a practitioner to tell us we are wrong. See the Division 36 challenge.
