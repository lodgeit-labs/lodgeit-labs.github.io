## 1. What this bounty is

You are being asked to read a small file of executable rules that state Division 36 of the *Income Tax Assessment Act 1997* — how a tax loss is calculated (s 36-10), how earlier-year tax losses are deducted (s 36-15), and how net exempt income (s 36-20) interacts with both. The rules are written in **Logical English** — a controlled natural-language layer over Prolog.

Your job is to tell us whether those rules **state the law correctly**. Not whether they run. Not whether they are efficient. Not whether the software architecture is sound. Just: **does the file, read as a statement of law, agree with the statute?**

The three attachments in the artefact bundle tell you everything you need to know to do this:

- **01 — How to read a Logical English rule.** A guide written for a tax practitioner with no technical background. Start there.
- **02 — The rules themselves.** About 90 lines. Plain text.
- **03 — Verdict form.** Eight questions. Prose in an email is equally acceptable.
