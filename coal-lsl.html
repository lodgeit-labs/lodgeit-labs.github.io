Coal LSL levy available external contributor

Monthly Coal Mining Industry long service leave payroll levy and eligible wages for one employee and one month. Eligible wages are the greater of Formula A and Formula B under s 3B of the Coal Mining Industry (Long Service Leave) Payroll Levy Collection Act 1992; the levy is that figure at the published 2.7% rate, rounded once at the end. The caller establishes the employee's eligibility and pay facts; the calculator does not assess them. Internal arithmetic is exact; money outputs are 2 dp, eligible wages and Formula B keep quarter cents at 4 dp, and levy_before_rounding is exact at 8 dp before the single final rounding to cents.

Hosted by the contributor, not on LodgeiT infrastructure. This calculator runs on the contributor's own Cloudflare Worker. LodgeiT points at it and attests conformance only — the numbers reproduce against a published standard and an inspectable source. It is not yet practitioner-attested: no credentialed practitioner has certified that the levy logic is correct against the statute. See the provenance panel below, and the open bounty seeking that review.

Endpoint

URN urn:sbrm:calculator:coal-lsl:levy, module urn:sbrm:module:coal-lsl. Period-scoped by month: the period URN is urn:sbrm:period:coal-lsl-levy:{YYYY-MM}, because levy returns are monthly. Supported months are January 2024 to August 2026. Selection depends on facts, not an election. Read the input schema from /openapi.json (OpenAPI 3.1) before calling.

Facts the selected branch needs but the caller has not supplied, and pay components the branch would discard, are refused with 400 and a stable refusal_class.

What the figures are

  • Eligible wages = greater of Formula A and Formula B (s 3B(1), base-rate branch). Formula A is the base rate of pay including incentive payments and bonuses. Formula B is 75% of (base rate + overtime and penalty rates + allowances other than expense reimbursements). The annual-salary and casual branches apply s 3B(2) and s 3B(3) respectively.
  • Levy = eligible wages × 2.7%, computed exactly then rounded once, half up to cents, in integer arithmetic. The rate is reviewer to verify against the current levy regulations — this page reports the 2.7% value the contributor's pinned source uses (methods.json, record s3b-from-2024-01), not an independently re-derived statutory rate.
  • Scales: money 2 dp; rates and factors 4 dp; eligible wages and Formula B 4 dp (exact quarter cents); levy_before_rounding exact at 8 dp. Each amount is capped at 833999930994.53, with an aggregate limit checked separately.
  • Each computed response carries rate and method content hashes, statutory citations, engine source hash, version and code_revision. No calculation fetches external data or uses the current date.
  • Statutory basis: s 3B of the Coal Mining Industry (Long Service Leave) Payroll Levy Collection Act 1992, as amended by the Fair Work Legislation Amendment (Protecting Worker Entitlements) Act 2023, read in Compilation No. 12. The source check in the manifest is automated retrieval, not a practitioner attestation.

Worked example

One eligible employee, base-rate branch, June 2026, where Formula B wins and the levy lands on a half-cent (so the final rounding bites):

curl -s -X POST \
  "https://coal-lsl-levy.coal-lsl-levy-service.workers.dev/v1/calculators/urn:sbrm:calculator:coal-lsl:levy/urn:sbrm:period:coal-lsl-levy:2026-06" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "reporting_month": "2026-06",
    "branch": "base_rate",
    "employee": { "eligible_employee": true },
    "pay": {
      "base_rate_of_pay": "6000.00",
      "salary_sacrificed": "0.00",
      "overtime_and_penalty_rates": "3000.00",
      "allowances": "500.00",
      "allowances_exclude_expense_reimbursements": true,
      "bonuses": []
    }
  }'

Derivation: Formula A = 6000.00. Formula B = 0.75 × (6000.00 + 3000.00 + 500.00) = 0.75 × 9500.00 = 7125.00. The greater is 7125.00. Levy = 7125.00 × 0.027 = 192.375, rounded half up to 192.38. From the live response:

branch.code              "s 3B(1)"
eligible_wages           "7125.0000"

workings.winner          "formula_b"
rate.value_percent       "2.7000"
levy_before_rounding     "192.37500000"
levy                     "192.38"
rounding.differs         true

This is one of 8 computed fixtures; the suite also exercises 5 factual refusals (unknown eligibility, not eligible, casual loading unanswered, casual components not read, allowances without the reimbursement assertion), 8 validation failures, and 2 unsupported-period 404s.
