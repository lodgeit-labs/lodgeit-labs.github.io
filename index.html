The Scene Protocol

Specification · In Progress

Ledgers are caches. Activity is the truth.

Every trade is one shared story — commitment, fulfilment, claim, settlement, receipt — and each party's accounting system holds only a cached projection of it. The Scene Protocol supplies the trust layer that turns projection into replay: one shared record of the scene, and any ledger derivable from it as a pure, verifiable function.
